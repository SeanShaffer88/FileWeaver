# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

Everyone participating in the FileWeaver project — issues, pull requests, and discussions — is expected to help keep the space welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background or identity.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive; critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and explain disagreements with evidence.
- Accept review decisions gracefully; maintainers may close contributions that do not fit the project's scope or safety rules.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory language of any kind.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Spam, trolling, or deliberate disruption of project spaces.

## Enforcement

The maintainers (see [MAINTAINERS.md](MAINTAINERS.md)) may remove comments, close issues, or block participants who violate this code, at their discretion. Reports can be made through the GitHub issue tracker or directly to a maintainer; reports will be handled as privately as the situation allows.

This is a short-form code, adapted from the spirit of the Contributor Covenant.
