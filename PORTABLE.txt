FileWeaver — portable build
===========================

No installation needed: unzip this archive anywhere and run
fileweaver.exe.

Where your data lives
---------------------
FileWeaver keeps its operation ledger (the record of applied jobs that
makes rollback possible) in a "data" folder beside the executable:
data\operations.sqlite. Nothing is written to %APPDATA%, the registry,
or anywhere else. If the folder beside the executable cannot be written,
the ledger falls back to the per-user location
%LOCALAPPDATA%\FileWeaver\operations.sqlite instead.

Erasing your data
-----------------
Run `fileweaver --clean` (or delete the data folder) to remove the
ledger and its history. Rollback history is lost when the ledger is
deleted.

Requirements
------------
Windows 10 (build 19041) or later, x64. The executable is self-contained:
no separate runtime install is required.
